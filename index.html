Custom Electronics for Harsh Environments

Rugged, energy-efficient systems designed to sense, track, and communicate taken from prototype to production

WindTag Sample Engagement Flow

The device and system were taken from a quick sketch on a piece of paper to field-proven hardware and software acquired by Tagmarshal in early 2025.

Concept Discovery

August 2023

It starts with a sketch. Whether it’s on a napkin or in your notebook, we help refine your idea into something real—mapping out feasibility, constraints, and the earliest signs of system architecture.

Early Prototyping

October 2023

We move fast by staying scrappy. Using breakout boards, scavenged parts, and bits from past projects, we build something you can touch and test early. This phase is all about proving the concept under real constraints—mechanical, electrical, and environmental. It doesn’t have to be pretty. It has to work.

Hardware Design

January 2023

Once the prototype proves it’s worth pursuing, we translate what we’ve learned into a custom PCB—optimized for power, size, durability, and deployment. This is where your solution stops looking hacked together and starts becoming the product.

Integration & Pre-Deployment Testing

March 2024

This is where everything starts coming together. We integrate the electronics into their intended form and begin testing early iterations in the field—seeing how they hold up to the real world, not just the bench. It’s where we learn what breaks, what holds, and what needs refining before scale.

Iteration

July 2024

Initial deployment is the beginning of iteration. With the hardware now in its real environment, we log performance, spot edge-case failures, and roll out rapid board and enclosure tweaks. Each iteration shaves power, hardens mechanics, and sharpens data quality until the device is production-ready.

Refinement

December 2024

After multiple field iterations, the hardware is refined for performance, reliability, and manufacturability. Enclosures are ruggedized, battery life is dialed in, and everything is streamlined for real-world deployment at scale.

Support, Launch & Scale

January 2025

Once your system is field-proven, we help you take it further—whether that’s low-volume builds, product documentation, on-site support, or industry rollouts. We stay engaged beyond the handoff, backing your success as your product grows into the market.
