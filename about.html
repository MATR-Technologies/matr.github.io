Our Expertise

MATR designs low-power, field-ready electronics that sense, track, and communicate in places conventional gear can’t survive. As a small, focused engineering team, we only take on projects we can fully commit to—because we believe solving hard problems takes full attention, not divided bandwidth. When commercial off-the-shelf solutions fall short, require modification, or simply can’t survive your conditions, we dive in completely. From remote weather stations to mesh-connected field sensors and asset trackers in dead zones, we bring clarity, precision, and deep technical execution to every challenge we take on.
